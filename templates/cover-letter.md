# Cover letter template

Keep it to one page. Editors read it in under a minute. Replace every bracket; delete sections the journal does not ask for.

---

[Date]

Dear Editor [or named Editor-in-Chief],

Please consider our manuscript, "[Title]", for publication in [Journal] as a [full-length article / letter / short communication].

[One paragraph, two to four sentences: the problem, what we did, and the main result with a number. This is the contribution sentence from the proposal, expanded by one sentence of evidence.]

[One paragraph: why this journal. Name the readership and, if relevant, one or two recent papers in the journal that this work builds on or answers.]

We confirm that this work is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. [If applicable: An earlier, shorter version was presented at [conference] and published in its proceedings as [reference]; the present manuscript extends it with [what is new, in one sentence].] [If applicable: A preprint is available at [arXiv ID].] [If applicable: Part of this work forms part of the [Masters / PhD] thesis of [student name] at the University of Pretoria; the thesis will be deposited in the University's institutional repository (UPSpace) [and is / is not] embargoed. This does not constitute prior publication.] All authors have approved the manuscript and agree to its submission. We have no conflicts of interest to declare. [Or: declare them.]

[If the journal asks:] Suggested reviewers: [three to five names, affiliations, emails; no recent co-authors]. [If justified:] We request that [name] not be invited to review because [brief reason].

The corresponding author is [name], [email], ORCID [number].

Yours sincerely,

[Name]
[Position], Department of Mechanical and Aeronautical Engineering, University of Pretoria
on behalf of all authors
